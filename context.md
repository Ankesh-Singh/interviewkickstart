## 6. Lead Generation & Conversion Funnel
The site's primary conversion mechanism is the **FREE Live Market Insight & Career Webinar**:

### Multi-Step Webinar Booking Flow:
1. **Step 1: Contact Details**
   - Fields: Full Name, Email ID, Phone Number (with Country Code selector, e.g. +91, +1, etc.), WhatsApp updates opt-in checkbox, Privacy Policy acceptance.
2. **Step 2: Webinar Slot Selection**
   - Date picker, Time slot options (e.g., Sat 6:00 PM, Morning 6:00 AM), Time Zone indicator (e.g., Asia/Kolkata, Asia/Dhaka), Urgency cues ("Almost full", "Filling fast", "Limited seats left").
3. **Step 3: Professional Profile Qualification**
   - Years of Experience: `0-2`, `3-4`, `5-8`, `9-15`, `16-20`, `20+`, or `Student`.
   - Domain / Current Role: Backend, Full Stack, Frontend, ML/AI, Data Engineering, Data Science, EM, TPM, Tech PM, Cloud/DevOps/SRE, Cyber Security, SDET, Mobile (iOS/Android), Embedded, Data Analyst, etc.
   - Status toggles: "I have been laid off recently", "I'm currently a student".
   - Career Goal / Next Move: Add AI skills, Learn ML & crack top roles, Build Agentic AI portfolio with PwC, Prepare for MAANG+ interviews.
   - Interview Timeline: `Already interviewing`, `<30 days`, `30 - 60 days`, `>60 days`, `No plans as of yet`.
   - Free Consultation Call Preference: Morning (9 AM–12 PM), Afternoon (12 PM–5 PM), Evening (5 PM–8 PM).
   - Optional Value Add: LinkedIn review (URL input), Resume review (PDF upload up to 4MB).
4. **Step 4: Confirmation & Next Steps**
   - Success state confirming booked webinar slot, callback advisor schedule, and calendar invites.
