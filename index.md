## FAQ

Program Structure & Value
Enrollment & Prerequisites
Learning Outcomes

What is the duration of the programs?

We have multiple offerings with varied timelines. You should be able to find a program that suits your needs.

- Transformative GenAI program: 3 months
- Flagship Machine Learning program: 6+4 months
- Agentic AI Career Accelerator program: 12 weeks
- Interview Prep Lab: 3 months

How much time do I need to dedicate to the programs?

The programs are rigorous. We expect a level of commitment from your end to allow you to fully leverage all the benefits we have to offer. Our successful alumni have typically dedicated a few hours a day, daily, to work through the prep material, videos, lectures, and tests. That said, we understand that your schedule might not allow you to dedicate the time daily. Hence, for all programs, we will offer you study materials and 1×1 technical and career coaching sessions during our long support period.

Where are the classes held?

Our classes are conducted live, online along with self-paced recorded videos. Classes are scheduled to primarily serve working professionals.

Do you guarantee placement if I enroll in one of your programs?

We have multiple programs on offer at different price points. The easiest way to find out about them would be to register for our free webinar where we discuss the various programs and their pricing.

How do I enroll?

We run free market insight webinars for our 3 kinds of courses–AI pivot, Agentic upskilling, and interview prep. Just attend the webinar most aligned with your career goals, and a Program Advisor would reach out to you to help you start.

Is attending the pre-enrollment webinar mandatory?

Yes! The programs we offer are elaborate with a lot of nuances. We want to make sure you hear about them from the horse’s mouth, clearly and completely, in the pre-enrollment webinar. The webinar is an interactive online session where you can meet one of our founders and directly ask questions (if any) so that there are no surprises later. The broad agenda is.

- Market Insights
- Who we are
- How our programs work
- How to get the most out of the program
- What are the costs
- How to enroll
- Q & A

Which position/role are the programs helpful for?

Our programs are suitable for all tech professionals, such as developers, software engineers, product managers, technical program managers, and engineering managers. We also have courses for DevOps, Site Reliability Engineers, Cloud Solutions Architects, and Cyber Security professionals. We cover multiple software engineering domains, such as Fullstack Engineer, Backend Engineer, Server Engineer, Software Engineer, Data Engineer, Platform Engineer, API Developer, Automation Engineer, Android Developer, iOS Developer, and more.

What programming languages do I need to know before I enroll for the program?

You should know AT LEAST ONE programming language of your choice. Typically, it would be your primary language used in your domain expertise. Our program is language-agnostic for the most part. For Machine Learning courses, we work with Python, but you will get Python learning material even if you don’t already know it.

Is any prior preparation required before I enroll for the program?

Yes, only if you have time and you want to. It is not mandatory. If you want to prep between the time you enroll and the start of your cohort, we will be happy to send you a list of pre-work material.

How is your coaching different from me learning on my own?

Our programs are designed for working professionals–with live and flexible classes that fit into your schedule, so you can upgrade your skills without hitting pause on your career.

Here’s what makes us different:

- Hands-on Learning: Attend real-time classes, work on live projects and assignments, and explore self-paced projects to solidify your learning.
- Direct Career Impact: Every skill you gain can be applied immediately to your current projects or used to pivot into new, AI-driven roles.
- Mock Interviews with Real Hiring Managers: Practice with FAANG+ interviewers and get targeted feedback to sharpen your edge.
- Long-Term Support: Access 6 months of post-course guidance and merit-based placement support to land the right role.
- Depth Over Fluff: Go beyond surface-level tutorials and prompt engineering sessions to master the latest and best industry practices.
- Tailored Learning Tracks: Train for your exact tech domain–be it backend, full stack, ML, PM, or others–so you get content that’s relevant, not generic.
- Proven Interview Strategies: Learn frameworks and best practices used by FAANG+ engineers, customized to your domain and career goals.

In short, we help you future-proof your career with AI-ready skills and industry-tested strategies

What are the goals of your programs?
